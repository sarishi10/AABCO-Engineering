Company introduction

AABCO Engineering (M) Sdn.Bhd

Established in 2003, AABCO is a specialist in the manufacturing of industrial tools, welding jigs & fixtures, fabrication, CNC machining, and assembly for demanding industrial applications.

With over two decades of manufacturing experience, we provide end-to-end manufacturing solutions, from fabrication and CNC machining through to assembly, inspection and final delivery.
